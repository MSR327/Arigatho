## 14 Nov 2025 · SDE · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online assessment, two technical coding interviews, a hiring-manager technical plus HR round, and an HR round.

### Online assessment

90 minutes, 3 coding questions, medium difficulty.

**Tricky problem**

One tricky problem. Exact question not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Math and binary search problem**

One problem focused on math and binary search. Exact question not given.

<sub>Topics: Math, Binary Search</sub>

**Pure implementation problem**

One pure implementation problem. Exact question not given.

<sub>Topics: Arrays</sub>

### Technical interview 1 (DS)

Code on Google Docs, about 30 minutes.

**Topological sort question with follow-ups**

A topological sort problem, dry run on the given test case, plus two follow-ups and an optional harder bonus follow-up.

<sub>Topics: Graphs</sub>

### Technical interview 2 (problem solving)

CoderPad, 65 minutes.

**LeetCode medium already solved**

A LeetCode medium the candidate had seen before; they disclosed it. Exact problem not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Second problem solved together and optimised**

A second problem worked out with the interviewer, then asked to optimise. Exact problem not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

### Technical plus hiring manager

Over an hour with an engineering manager.

**Cryptography project**

Explain the cryptography project on the CV: the scheme, optimisation and implementation.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Computer networks**

Protocols and layers, and what happens when a URL is searched.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**Databases and scaling**

Where databases are used, why NoSQL, why a graph DB, what scaling is and how it is done.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**System design concepts**

Sharding, partitioning and caches.

<sub>Topics: Software Engineering</sub>

**Operating systems**

Processes, threads, multithreading, mutexes, deadlocks, semaphores, deadlock examples and handling.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**Efficient querying from a DB**

How to perform efficient querying from a database; candidate answered with a trie and sharding.

<sub>Topics: Software Engineering, Other</sub>

**Tight deadline on unfamiliar feature**

What to do when given a feature with a tight deadline and no prior implementation knowledge.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR round

About 15 minutes.

**Why Myntra and career plans**

Why Myntra, career aspirations, where they see themselves in 5 years, and how Myntra differs from other e-commerce giants.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>
