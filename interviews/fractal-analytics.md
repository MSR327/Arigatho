## 5 Aug 2026 · Imagineer · VIT Chennai · on-campus

Pre-placement talk, proctored online assessment, two technical interviews, one HR interview.

### Online assessment

About 90 minutes, proctored

**Aptitude and technical sections**

Quantitative, logical and analytical aptitude (one answer explained on video) plus Python, Java, SQL and general programming; SQL needed full queries. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, SQL, OA Format</sub>

### Technical interview 1

About 45 minutes on HackerRank

**SQL spending queries**

Find the maximum amount spent; find the customer with the highest total spend in August 2025; find customers whose August 2025 total exceeded 10000. Follow-ups on why GROUP BY, ORDER BY, HAVING instead of WHERE, and query execution flow.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Relational vs non-relational databases**

Explain the difference between relational and non-relational databases; also a basic Python coding question.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ, Python & Pandas</sub>

**Resume and project**

Explain one project, the APIs used, and technologies listed on the resume.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Guesstimates**

How many cricket balls fit in an airplane; how many Domino's pizzas are ordered in Chennai per day.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Technical interview 2

About 20 to 25 minutes, online

**Major project deep dive**

Architecture, tech stack, backend, database design, deployment, APIs, challenges, future improvements.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR interview

About 20 minutes

**HR questions**

Why Fractal; hackathon experience; extracurriculars; a challenging situation; teamwork.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>
