## 3 Nov 2025 · Software Engineer · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online assessment, then five interviews: project, technical, puzzle, director, HR.

### Online assessment

Coding problems plus SQL queries.

**Coding and SQL**

A few coding problems and SQL query questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OA Format, SQL</sub>

### Project and technical interview

Short, casual.

**Projects and internships**

Questions on projects and internships on the CV. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### In-depth technical interview

About 45 minutes.

**Internship pseudocode**

Write pseudocode for processes implemented during internships.

<sub>Topics: Software Engineering</sub>

**OOP concepts**

Questions on OOP concepts. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OOP</sub>

**Second-highest salary**

Write an SQL query for the second-highest salary in an employee table.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Two medium DP problems**

Two medium-level LeetCode DP problems. Exact problems not given.

<sub>Topics: DP</sub>

**CPU scheduling**

OS concepts of FCFS and Round Robin scheduling.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

### Analytical and puzzle round

**Five boxes and a puppy**

A puppy hides in one of 5 boxes and moves to an adjacent box each night; devise a strategy to find it.

<sub>Topics: Puzzles</sub>

### Director round

Conversational.

**Interests and background**

General questions on interests, hobbies and personal background. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR round

**Location and standard HR**

Job location preference and standard HR questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practice LeetCode medium-hard and contests · Study DBMS and OS · Solve puzzles (e.g. Brainstellar) and prepare standard HR answers
