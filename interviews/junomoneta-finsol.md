## 19 Nov 2025 · Quantitative Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV shortlist, aptitude test, a technical interview, then an HR interview.

### Aptitude test

About 40 questions in 45 minutes, easy to medium, speed and accuracy mattered.

**Aptitude questions**

Quant-style aptitude questions, roughly CAT quant level. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude</sub>

### Technical interview

About 20 minutes.

**Why a quant role from a non-circuital branch**

Asked why they applied for a quant role despite a non-circuital branch and why they should be hired.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Rapid-fire DS, ML and statistics**

Rapid-fire basic questions on data structures, machine learning and statistics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI, Math</sub>

**CV grilling on finance and ML research project**

Detailed questions on a finance plus ML research project from the CV.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral, ML/AI</sub>

### HR interview

**Career vision and immediate goals**

Asked about career vision and immediate goals.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practise CAT quant and aptitude problems for about a month. · Know probability, statistics and ML models with their metrics and maths. · Be able to explain every CV item clearly.

## 25 Sep 2025 · Quantitative Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online CAT-style aptitude test (about 30 shortlisted), technical interview with two senior traders, HR chat.

### Online aptitude test

CAT-style quant, many questions in limited time.

**CAT-style quant questions**

Many quantitative aptitude questions to be solved in limited time. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Math</sub>

### Technical interview

Two senior traders; resume deep dive.

**Strategies used at previous trading internship**

What strategies the candidate used during a prior trading internship.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Personal investing**

Whether the candidate invests personally.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Validating a physics-meets-finance model**

How the candidate validated their quantum-physics-inspired financial model.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

### HR chat

**Five-year plan**

Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practise CAT papers, probability and puzzles (Brainstellar). · Learn market basics and use a quant-finance interview book.
