## 14 Jan 2026 · Advanced Application Engineering Analyst · on-campus

Online assessment, verbal assessment, one technical interview.

### Online assessment

Gaming, cognitive and technical MCQ stages, then coding

**Coding section**

One medium SQL question, one backend question, one easy frontend question (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) with three tasks.

<sub>Topics: SQL, Web Dev, OA Format</sub>

### Verbal assessment

Read words aloud while recorded; score affected role and package

No specific questions reported.

### Technical interview

About 40 minutes, on Accenture web portal

**Profile and projects**

LeetCode profile discussion, final-year capstone backend work, Android (Kotlin) projects, tech stack, database design, architecture. Exact technical questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Build original projects; interviewers probe stack and design. · Pick an early interview slot. · Practice DSA consistently.

## 29 Dec 2025 · Associate Application Software Engineer · on-campus

Online assessment (behavioral, technical MCQ, coding), communication assessment, then a final online interview.

### Behavioral and cognitive

50 situational questions plus game-based tasks (maze, patterns)

No specific questions reported.

### Technical MCQs

Moderate

**Technical MCQ areas**

Computer Networks, Cloud Computing, Pseudocode, Software Development concepts. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

### Coding

Three tasks

**Frontend, SQL, DSA**

A frontend task in HTML, CSS and JavaScript; a join-based SQL query on a given schema; one backend problem on dynamic sliding window.

<sub>Topics: Web Dev, SQL, Sliding Window</sub>

### Communication assessment

Taken from home

No specific questions reported.

### Final interview

Online

**Project and HR**

Why this project and tech stack; how query performance was improved; architecture decisions; why Accenture; family background.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Keep PAN details current before registering. · Test internet and interview link beforehand. · Know your project design choices.

## 16 Sep 2025 · Industry & Functions AI Decision Science Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

MCQ online assessment, then two technical interviews of 30-45 minutes each.

### Online assessment

MCQ-based, about 1 to 1.5 hours.

**Aptitude and data science MCQs**

Covered aptitude, logical reasoning, Python, machine learning, deep learning, SQL and other data science topics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, ML/AI, SQL</sub>

### Technical interviews 1 and 2

Two rounds of 30-45 minutes each; selection based on the final interview.

**Projects and data case studies**

Questions mostly on the candidate's projects and some data-related case study scenarios. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI, Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Tips:** Revise probability, statistics, linear algebra, Python, ML and DL theory. · Learn DSA early and read up on Generative AI.

Source: [SWG Foresight, IIT Kharagpur](https://medium.com/@swgiitkgp/foresight-2025-placement-at-accenture-akriti-sakshi-42751db20a2d)
