## 31 Oct 2025 · R&D Engineer · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV shortlist, a 2-hour test, 2 to 3 technical interviews, one HR round.

### Test

2 hours: aptitude, basic encoder questions and logical mechanical questions.

**Aptitude, encoder and mechanical logic**

Aptitude plus basic encoder and logical mechanical questions needing knowledge of how sensors work. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical, Aptitude</sub>

### Technical interviews

2 to 3 rounds of 35 to 45 minutes, online.

**JEE-level physics and maths plus vague common-sense questions**

Advanced 11th/12th level physics and maths (differential equations, work-energy-power, probability) and vague common-sense questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical, Math</sub>

**CV and core mechanical subjects**

Interviewers probe the CV and core subjects such as thermodynamics, IC engines, fluid mechanics, heat transfer. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

### HR round

About 20 minutes, offline.

**Career plans and cultural fit**

Career plans and extracurricular background; talk of civil services, MBA or foreign study can hurt. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Know working principles of common sensors and actuators. · Prepare thermodynamics plus one more core subject like solids or manufacturing.

## 30 Sep 2025 · R&D Engineer · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online MCQ assessment, two online interviews where problems were solved on paper, then a short offline HR round.

### Online assessment

MCQ test of about 1.5 hours at Nalanda; fundamentals of mechanical and electrical plus aptitude, minimal calculation.

**Mechanical, electrical and aptitude MCQs**

Fundamentals in mechanical and electrical domains plus aptitude. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical, Circuits, Aptitude</sub>

### Interview 1

About 45 minutes on Zoom with a senior R&D engineer; solve on blank paper, process matters more than answer.

**RC circuit question**

One subjective question from RC circuits. Exact statement not given.

<sub>Topics: Circuits</sub>

**Fluids: gravity flow**

One subjective question on the gravity flow concept in fluids. Exact statement not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Torque and shafts**

One subjective question on torque and shafts. Exact statement not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

About 2 hours 15 minutes with the R&D lead engineer; about 15 subjective questions on paper, unusual in style.

**Mixed subjective questions**

Around 15 questions mixing mathematical aptitude, mechanical concepts, probability, electrical resistance arrangements and mathematical derivations. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Mechanical, Circuits</sub>

**Rough code for assigning probability**

Write rough code for assigning probability. Further details not given.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

### HR round

Offline, about 10 minutes.

**Ethics and morals at work**

Simple background questions, including the importance of ethics and morals in the workplace.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Revise GATE-level mechanical theory and solve GATE PYQs. · Practise aptitude (IndiaBIX, CAT PYQs) and JEE-level electrical concepts. · Questions repeat, so search for previous questions.
