## 2025-26 season · Business Analyst I · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Aptitude test and resume shortlisting, then three rounds: resume plus table-based questions with SQL, a short RCA interview, and an HR interview.

### Aptitude test and resume shortlisting

No specific questions reported.

### Round 1

Resume questions, questions on a sheet of tabular data, and a pen-and-paper SQL query of easy to medium level on the given tables.

**SQL query on given tables**

Write an easy-to-medium SQL query using the tables provided, on pen and paper. Exact question not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Random forest and OBE**

Explain random forest and what OBE (out-of-bag error) is in a random forest.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### Round 2

Short interview with an RCA question.

**RCA: 10% drop in cab bookings**

You work for an online cab service and notice bookings dropped by 10%. What could be the possible reasons?

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Round 3

HR interview.

No specific questions reported.

**Tips:** For BA roles interviewers focus on the resume rather than deep technical questions; a finance background helped. · Communicate clearly and confidently.

## 29 Nov 2024 · Data Scientist · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online assessment with MCQs, SQL and coding, then one interview round.

### Online assessment

About 20 MCQs in 25 minutes, 2 SQL questions in 10 minutes, 2 coding problems in 35 minutes of easy to medium level.

**ML, probability, aptitude and statistics MCQs**

About 20 MCQs in 25 minutes covering theoretical ML, probability, aptitude and statistics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI, Math, Aptitude</sub>

**SQL questions**

Two SQL questions in 10 minutes. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Two coding problems**

Two LeetCode easy-to-medium coding problems in 35 minutes. Exact problems not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

### Interview

Single round; CV discussed only lightly.

**Binary search problem**

A DSA problem involving binary search. Exact problem not given.

<sub>Topics: Binary Search</sub>

**Nearest greater element index**

A variation of finding, for each array element, the index of the nearest greater element.

<sub>Topics: Arrays, Stack/Queue</sub>

**Probability and ML theory**

Questions on probability and basic theoretical ML concepts. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Math, ML/AI</sub>

**Tips:** Prepare DSA, ML theory, SQL and probability-statistics (hypothesis testing, CLT, distributions, p-value). · Have 2-3 ML projects or internships on the CV.
