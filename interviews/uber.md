### Gateway round

1 hour: 45 minutes coding, 15 minutes high-level design

**Minimum transitions between 4-digit nodes**

Nodes are 4-digit codes; two connect if they differ in exactly one digit, and moves are limited to an activated list containing the start. Return the minimum transitions to a target or -1. Similar to Word Ladder; solved with BFS.

<sub>Topics: Graphs</sub>

### Coding round 1 (DSA)

1 hour

**Earliest time everyone is connected**

Find the earliest time at which everyone in a network becomes connected, similar to Earliest Moment When Everyone Become Friends; solved with Union-Find.
