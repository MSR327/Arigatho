## 2025-26 season · Edison Engineer · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Resume shortlist, an easy written assessment, then a technical interview and a long HR interview.

### Resume shortlist

Good CPI and relevant projects help.

No specific questions reported.

### Written assessment

Easy; basics of mechanical subjects and aptitude.

No specific questions reported.

### Technical interview

15-20 minutes.

**Strength of materials and dynamics basics**

Questions on strength of materials and basics of dynamics.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Thesis and coursework**

Questions about the thesis and courses taken, and a challenge to convince them the thesis fits despite not aligning with their work.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR interview

50-60 minutes.

**Something not on your CV**

Tell me something that is not in your CV.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Pressure, challenges, workplace issues, achievements, criticism**

How do you handle pressure and challenges, your biggest challenge, workplace issues, your achievements and criticism.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Design a cycle brake**

A case or analysis on designing a cycle brake.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Tips:** Research the company's recent milestones, vision and mission. · Take notes during the PPT and use them in the interview. · Do not bluff.
