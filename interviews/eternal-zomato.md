## 7 Nov 2025 · Product Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online test on SQL and aptitude with CV shortlisting, then three technical interviews and one HR round in a single offline day.

### Online test

Mostly SQL (intermediate to advanced) and fairly basic aptitude; CGPA cutoff 7.5.

**SQL and aptitude test**

Intermediate to advanced SQL questions plus basic aptitude. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL, Aptitude</sub>

### Technical interview 1

About 1.5 hours, offline, CV walked through line by line.

**ML concepts from projects**

Questions on PCA, bidirectional LSTM, XGBoost feature importance, LASSO regression and basic ML and stats.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Self-rate SQL then write queries**

Asked to rate SQL out of 10, then to write some fairly difficult SQL queries on paper. Exact queries not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

### Technical interview 2

About 2 hours.

**SQL concepts**

Conceptual SQL on joins, unions, order of execution, window functions (rank, dense_rank, frame bounds, lead, lag), pivoting with and without PIVOT, and grouping sets.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Favourite product**

Asked about favourite product (answered Instagram).

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Metrics for Instagram share feature**

What metrics would you track to measure success of the share feature, plus other Instagram metrics questions.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Technical interview 3

About 30 min, online, with a lead analyst.

**Shut down one of four cloud kitchens**

Business case on deciding whether to shut down one of four cloud kitchens in Bangalore.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Cloud kitchen performance metrics**

Product metrics case on what to track for cloud kitchen performance, focusing on logistics.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### HR round

Informal conversation on expectations from the company.

No specific questions reported.

**Tips:** Practice SQL, especially window functions, pivots and grouping sets. · Practice product metrics, RCA and product sense cases with peers. · Stay calm and honest; show your approach.
