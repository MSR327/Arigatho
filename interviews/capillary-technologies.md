## 11 Nov 2025 · SDE-1 · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online test, offline written test, three technical interviews, one HR interview.

### Online test

Mettle platform, timed sections.

**Aptitude, probability and English sections**

Basic to medium aptitude, probability and English sections. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Verbal</sub>

**Two coding questions**

Two LeetCode-medium level coding questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OA Format</sub>

### Offline written test

Two coding questions on paper.

**Two graph coding problems**

Two graph-based coding questions, solved with DFS/traversal logic. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Graphs</sub>

### Technical interview 1

Online, 1 hr 15 min, screen shared, Codeshare.

**Explain written-test solutions**

Explain both solutions written in the offline test.

<sub>Topics: Graphs</sub>

**BST traversal and sum of nodes**

Code BST traversal, sum of nodes and similar tasks; all questions on BSTs and graphs.

<sub>Topics: Trees, Graphs</sub>

### Technical interview 2

Online, 1 hr 15 min.

**SQL basics, joins, subqueries**

Started with basic SQL then joins and subqueries for about half an hour.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**SQL normalisation**

Questions on normalisation.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Networks and OS concepts**

Questions on TCP, UDP, memory leaks and deadlocks. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**System architecture**

Some questions on system architecture such as microservices.

<sub>Topics: Software Engineering</sub>

### Technical interview 3

Online, 1 hr, mostly CV discussion.

**CV, internships and BTP**

Discussion of resume, internships and BTP project.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Minimum platforms**

Given train arrival and departure times, find the minimum number of platforms required.

<sub>Topics: Greedy, Sorting</sub>

**Two puzzles**

Two puzzles were asked. Exact puzzles not given.

<sub>Topics: Puzzles</sub>

**General questions**

Questions on vision, interests and experiences.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR interview

Offline, about 40 min, friendly conversation.

**Intro, experiences, hobbies, college life**

Introduction, experiences, hobbies and college life questions.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practice competitive programming and LeetCode, with focus on graphs and BSTs. · Revise OOP, SQL, networks and OS. · Write only the core logic in written tests, not boilerplate.
