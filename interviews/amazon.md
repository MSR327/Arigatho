## 18 Sep 2026 · SDE-1 · off-campus

Online assessment then four interview rounds incl. bar raiser; offer via referral. Author had about 1 year as a data science intern.

### Online assessment

Two coding problems plus work-style and work-simulation

**OA coding**

One medium binary search problem and one medium-hard DP problem. Exact statements not given.

<sub>Topics: Binary Search, DP</sub>

### Round 1: DSA and LP

**Rotten Oranges variation**

Multi-source BFS.

<sub>Topics: Graphs · Similar: [LeetCode 994. Rotting Oranges](https://leetcode.com/problems/rotting-oranges/) (Medium)</sub>

**Search in rotated sorted array**

Binary search in a rotated sorted array.

<sub>Topics: Binary Search · Similar: [LeetCode 33. Search in Rotated Sorted Array](https://leetcode.com/problems/search-in-rotated-sorted-array/) (Medium)</sub>

### Round 2: DSA and LP

**Currency conversion as a graph**

BFS/DFS, then weighted DSU for many queries.

<sub>Topics: Graphs</sub>

**Decode String**

Iterative stack solution, needed hints.

<sub>Topics: Stack/Queue, Strings · Similar: [LeetCode 394. Decode String](https://leetcode.com/problems/decode-string/) (Medium)</sub>

### Round 3: SDM and AI fluency

**Using LLMs at work**

How LLMs are used, how responses are verified and trusted.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Two knights on an infinite chessboard**

BFS, then optimisation questions.

<sub>Topics: Graphs · Similar: [LeetCode 1197. Minimum Knight Moves](https://leetcode.com/problems/minimum-knight-moves/) (Medium)</sub>

### Bar raiser

Mostly LP, 40-45 minutes

**Binary string transformation**

Repeatedly map 0 to 00 and 1 to 10; find the value at index i after k transformations. Not solved optimally.

<sub>Topics: Recursion/Backtracking, Bit Manipulation</sub>

## 15 Sep 2026 · SDE-1 New Grad (AWS)

**Path Sum II with harder variation**

<sub>Topics: Trees, Recursion/Backtracking · Similar: [LeetCode 113. Path Sum II](https://leetcode.com/problems/path-sum-ii/) (Medium)</sub>

Behavioral question on a blocker and how it was handled.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Billing service for one AWS customer**

<sub>Topics: OOP, Software Engineering</sub>

**First non-repeating character**
