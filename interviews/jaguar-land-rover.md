## 29 Nov 2024 · Graduate Mechatronics Engineer Trainee · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

ERP application, three-part test, then a 1.5-hour interview including 15 minutes of HR.

### Online test

Three parts.

**Aptitude, technical and software tests**

Aptitude test; a technical test (mechanical for mechanical students, electrical and electronics for EE/ECE); and a software test of 2-3 DSA question sets. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Mechanical, OA Format</sub>

### Interview

1.5 hours with about 15 minutes of HR.

**CV and vehicle topics**

Mostly CV questions, plus vehicle dynamics, electric vehicle architecture, control systems and internal combustion engines. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical, Control Systems</sub>

**HR questions**

About 15 minutes of HR questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Keep limited resources and revise them regularly · Use a GATE PYQ book and a test series
