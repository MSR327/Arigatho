## 2025-26 season · Analytics Consultant · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

An aptitude test, then a consulting-style interview and an HR interview with puzzles.

### Aptitude test

Mental ability, quantitative aptitude, logical reasoning and puzzles.

No specific questions reported.

### Round 1: consulting

Guesstimates, puzzles, resume projects, then SQL joins; asked whether a Python analytics project could be replicated on Google Colab.

**Cars moving in Delhi**

Estimate the number of cars currently moving on the roads of Delhi at this hour.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Fish in a lake**

Estimate the number of fish in a lake with length and breadth 4 m and height 6 m.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Types of SQL joins**

What are the types of joins? Explain each with an example.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

### Round 2: HR

Behavioural questions plus two puzzles; focus on communication and breaking down a problem.

**Measure 4 L with 3 L and 5 L containers**

With containers of 3 L and 5 L, how do you measure 4 L?

<sub>Topics: Puzzles</sub>

**Man in a 50 m well**

A man in a 50 m well climbs 4 m each day and slips 3 m each night; in how many days does he get out?

<sub>Topics: Puzzles</sub>

**Average salary of three people**

Three people each earn an unknown salary and none will reveal it; how do you estimate the average?

<sub>Topics: Puzzles</sub>

**Drop and IIT versus another college**

Was taking a drop and coming to IIT a better choice than joining an NIT or private college?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Start preparing at the start of 7th semester; the resume is the first thing tested. · Target two or three roles rather than all of them. · Used Brainstellar, GFG, IIM casebooks and the LeetCode SQL 50.

Source: [SPO Insights, IIT Kanpur](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BzzvjG41dseyF7wQR4As_lbFoXLvI08O/view)
