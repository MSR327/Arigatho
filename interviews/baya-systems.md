## 2025-26 season · Software Engineer · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Online DSA test, a DSA interview round, then a company-specific second round (reported across several SDE and systems companies).

### Online test

DSA questions; difficulty varied by company; shortlists came very late in the season.

No specific questions reported.

### Round 1

DSA, decent but not very hard.

**DSA problems**

Basic Striver, CSES or Codeforces problems rated below about 1400-1500. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Arrays, Graphs, DP</sub>

### Round 2

Company specific; a database company asks DBMS, general SDE companies ask OS/OOP, some (including Baya Systems) ask C++ design.

**OS, OOP and DBMS fundamentals**

Questions on OS, OOP and DBMS depending on the company. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ, OOP</sub>

**High-level design in C++**

Give a high-level design of a functionality: which data structures, objects and classes you would choose.

<sub>Topics: Software Engineering, OOP</sub>

**Tips:** DSA, OOP and OS are asked everywhere; some companies now ask system design in detail. · Steer the interview toward your strong topic and prepare it deeply. · Plan the busy day-one slots ahead because shortlists come late.
