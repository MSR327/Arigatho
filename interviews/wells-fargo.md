## 30 Sep 2025 · Quantitative Program Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV shortlisting, online test, then three eliminative offline interviews on Day 2.

### Online test

Three sections.

**Data interpretation section**

Data interpretation questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude</sub>

**Maths, probability and statistics section**

Maths with probability and statistics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**Two coding questions**

Two basic DSA problems, LeetCode easy to medium. Exact problems not given.

<sub>Topics: OA Format</sub>

### Interview 1

About 40-45 minutes, CV grilling.

**SQL and pandas basics**

Basic SQL and pandas questions focused on approach to querying different kinds of datasets, not syntax. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL, Python & Pandas</sub>

### Interview 2

About 1 hour 20 minutes.

**ML algorithms from CV**

Average-level ML questions on XGBoost and PCA, which were on the CV.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Probability problems**

Several probability questions, mostly from the book 50 Challenging Problems in Probability. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**FRM-related questions**

A few finance risk questions based on the FRM certification on the CV. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Data handling in Python and SQL**

Data handling questions in Python; the candidate redid them in SQL. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Python & Pandas, SQL</sub>

### HR round

Ended as a discussion of amenities and work-life balance.

**Typical HR questions**

Standard HR questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practise probability from 50 Challenging Problems or the Green Book, incl. Markov chains and expectation · Do basic DSA · Revise certification basics listed on the CV
