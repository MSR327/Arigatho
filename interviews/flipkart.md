## 5 Nov 2024 · Business Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV and CGPA screening, then two interview rounds: an ML-focused case study, then hypothesis testing, guesstimates and culture fit.

### Round 1

Case study applying machine learning.

**ML models on the CV**

Detailed questions on logistic regression, decision trees and K-nearest neighbors listed on the CV, including their underlying mathematics. The case itself varies by candidate.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### Round 2

**Hypothesis testing**

Questions on hypothesis testing. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**Guesstimates**

Guesstimate questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Project in Flipkart's mobile app**

Propose how a project could be implemented within Flipkart's mobile application.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Company culture and motivation**

Questions on Flipkart's company culture and why the candidate wants to join.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Have hands-on data internships and projects on the CV to steer the discussion. · Study ML basics, hypothesis testing, probability and statistics, and practise puzzles.
