### JTG coding assessment

Two DSA problems at the office

**Deepest level sum of a binary tree**

Given a binary tree, return the sum of nodes at its deepest level.

<sub>Topics: Trees</sub>

**Maximum sum of three increasing values**

Given an array of prices choose i&lt;j&lt;k with price[i]&lt;price[j]&lt;price[k] maximising their sum.

<sub>Topics: Arrays</sub>

### Technical interview 1 (second round)

Face to face, about 2 hours

**Project discussion**

Functionality, architecture, technologies, own contribution.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Minimum length subarray to sort**

Find the length of the smallest contiguous subarray that must be sorted for the whole array to be sorted; -1 if already sorted.

<sub>Topics: Arrays</sub>

**Longest consecutive path in a binary tree**

Longest downward path where each child value is exactly parent plus one.

<sub>Topics: Trees · Similar: [LeetCode 298. Binary Tree Longest Consecutive Sequence](https://leetcode.com/problems/binary-tree-longest-consecutive-sequence/) (Medium)</sub>

### Technical interview 2

Face to face, about 2 hours

**Project discussion**

Implementation and technical details of the project.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Maximum subarray sum over inorder traversal**

Take a binary tree's inorder traversal and find the maximum contiguous subarray sum (Kadane).

<sub>Topics: Trees, DP</sub>

**Split N into M positive parts**

Generate all ways to split N into exactly M positive integers summing to N.

<sub>Topics: Recursion/Backtracking</sub>
