## 2025-26 season · AI Scientist · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Test followed by four interview rounds (ML fundamentals, deep grilling, build an ML model from scratch, and a further round).

### Test

Python, ML, deep learning, statistics and linear algebra.

No specific questions reported.

### Round 1

Medium-level fundamentals.

**ML algorithms**

Linear regression assumptions and working, ensemble methods, in-depth ML algorithm questions.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Statistics**

Correlation, covariance, CLT, Z and T tests, expectations.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**Linear algebra**

Rank, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, transformations.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**RNNs and Transformers**

RNN architecture and differences; complete Transformer architecture with explanation.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Easy SQL group by**

A four-line query based on GROUP BY.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**Easy Python dictionary coding**

An easy coding problem based on a dictionary.

<sub>Topics: Hashing</sub>

### Round 2

In-depth grilling with mathematical derivations.

**Statistics in depth**

CLT, standard deviation, Bayes theorem, hypothesis testing.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**Transformer and BERT**

Explain the Transformer with mathematical intuition for each step, how BERT is trained, and train a transformer on an example.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**SVD derivation**

Derive singular value decomposition (recommender systems context), plus rank and matrices; deep questioning on linear algebra and statistics.

<sub>Topics: Math, ML/AI</sub>

### Round 3

**Build ML model from scratch**

Build an ML model from scratch without AI assistance.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### Round 4

Content not described.

No specific questions reported.
