## 5 Sep 2025 · Graduate Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

A written stock report of about 1500 words, CV and report shortlisting, an informal buddy chat, then seven interview rounds of increasing seniority ending with the founder and the CIO.

### Stock report submission

About 1500 words on a chosen stock: competitor, market landscape, sector analysis and outperformance potential; no valuation model required.

**Stock analysis report**

Pick a stock and analyse competitors, market landscape, sector and its potential to outperform peers, in about 1500 words.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

### Buddy interview

About 20 minutes, informal and not very technical.

**Why WhiteOak, not PE or IB**

Why WhiteOak and why not private equity or investment banking.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Interviews 1 to 7

Each 45-60 minutes; early rounds technical, later rounds a vibe check; 60-90% of questions came from the submitted report; HR scheduled each round after the last.

**Defend the report**

Detailed questions on every part of the submitted stock report; a financial-services interviewer was present in each round.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Rapid-fire valuation and accounting**

Basic rapid-fire valuation and accounting questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Tips:** Learn equity research, fundamental analysis, valuation and accounting; CFA Level 1 and 2 equity and accounting chapters help. · Read equity research reports and write your own for review. · Admit when you do not know an answer.
