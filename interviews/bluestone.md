## 18 Nov 2025 · Management Trainee · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Online aptitude test, group discussion, technical plus HR interview, managerial plus HR interview.

### Online test

6 sections of 7 multiple-choice questions each.

**Aptitude test**

MCQs on verbal reasoning, logical reasoning and data interpretation across 6 sections of 7 questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Verbal</sub>

### Group discussion

80 shortlisted, in 4 sets of 20; 3-4 minutes to note thoughts first.

**Jewellery company make vs outsource case**

A jewellery firm serves premium customers promised shorter delivery and non-premium customers, but lacks stock to meet demand. Decide whether to procure raw materials and manufacture in-house or outsource to a third-party vendor.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Technical + HR interview

Resume, coursework, a case, then HR in the last 10 minutes.

**Internship and project discussion**

In-depth questions on all projects, with 20-25 minutes on a factory-based internship.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Coursework questions**

Questions from quality control and management, inventory management and production planning. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Defective batch root cause case**

Manufacturing case where a recent batch had quality issues and defects; do a root cause analysis and propose solutions.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Why decline the PPO**

Why the candidate did not accept their earlier pre-placement offer.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Why Bluestone and higher-CTC follow-up**

Why join this company, and whether you would leave if another company offered higher pay after two months.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**MBA plans**

Whether you plan to pursue an MBA.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Managerial + HR interview

Similar to the previous round with deeper CV probing.

**CV, coursework and situational questions**

Detailed questions on all projects and internships, a few coursework questions and some situation-based questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Know every CV item well and only list courses you are comfortable with. · Make short revision notes for core subjects early. · Practise structuring thoughts for the GD.

## 5 Oct 2025 · Management Trainee, Supply Chain · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV submission, aptitude test, group discussion in panels of 10, two eliminative interviews with two-person panels.

### Aptitude test

Multiple timed sections.

**Reasoning, DI and verbal sections**

Logical reasoning, data interpretation, mathematical reasoning and verbal reasoning. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Verbal</sub>

### Group discussion

Panels of 10 students.

**Supply chain case and root cause analysis**

Supply chain cases focused on root cause analysis. Exact case not given.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Interview rounds

Two eliminative rounds, two-person panels.

**CV, supply chain cases, puzzles and HR**

Deep CV questions, supply chain case studies, puzzles and tricky HR questions. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral, Puzzles, Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Tips:** Practise supply chain and RCA cases and easy-medium puzzles. · Do mock interviews and prepare tricky HR questions.
