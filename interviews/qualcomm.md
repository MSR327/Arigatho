## 19 Oct 2025 · Engineer, Hardware · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Proctored online test with three sections, one technical interview, one HR round.

### Online proctored test

Three sections (Aptitude, Programming Basics, Hardware Core) of 20 questions and 30 minutes each; must submit a section to move on.

**Aptitude, programming and hardware sections**

20 questions each in aptitude, programming basics and core hardware. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Digital Electronics, OA Format</sub>

### Technical interview

Self-intro, CV questions, digital design, and a Verilog snippet on paper.

**Internship and project questions**

Questions on internships, projects and a Coursera computer architecture course listed on the CV.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Pipeline hazards and stages**

Explain pipeline hazards and the pipeline stages.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

**Clock divider by 8 and by 3**

Design clock frequency dividers using flip-flops, by factors of 8 and 3, with 50% duty cycle.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

**Flip-flop output and reset questions**

Determine the output of a given flip-flop circuit, how to clear garbage values in flip-flops, and pros and cons of asynchronous vs synchronous reset.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

**Setup time, hold time, mux-based D flip-flop, metastability**

Explain setup and hold time with causes and effects, the structure of a multiplexer-based D flip-flop, and metastability.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

**Latch vs flip-flop**

Differences between latches and flip-flops and where each is needed.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

**Write Verilog on paper**

Write a small Verilog code on pen and paper. Exact module not given.

<sub>Topics: Digital Electronics</sub>

### HR round

Formality.

No specific questions reported.

**Tips:** Revise digital electronics, computer architecture, Verilog and static timing analysis; FIFO depth calculation was useful for the test. · Practise timed aptitude and C++/Python output questions.
