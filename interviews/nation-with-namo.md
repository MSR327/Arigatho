## 9 Oct 2025 · GILP Associate · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Profile-tailored written test, then three eliminatory interviews.

### Written test

Held 15 to 20 days before interviews; formal attire required.

**Associate test sections**

Sections on general knowledge, current affairs, reading comprehension, verbal ability, quantitative aptitude and a statement of purpose. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Verbal</sub>

### Interview 1

**CV discussion**

Questions on the CV.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Coffee cups at IIT Kharagpur**

Estimate the number of coffee cups consumed daily at IIT Kharagpur.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Basic political knowledge**

Basic questions on politics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

### Interview 2

**Political case studies and elections**

Heavily politics-oriented: political case studies, elections and state politics (about 15 minutes on Jharkhand elections for this candidate). Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Another guesstimate**

One more guesstimate. Exact question not given.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### HR round

5 to 10 minutes.

**PoRs and internships**

Questions on positions of responsibility and internships.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Recall and solve round 1 guesstimate**

Asked to recall and solve the guesstimate from round 1.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Tips:** Follow current affairs and politics closely. · Practise guesstimates from the IIMA casebook. · Know your CV and do mocks with seniors.
