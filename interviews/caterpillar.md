## 2025-26 season · Associate Engineer · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

An online test (aptitude, reasoning, English and GATE-level core mechanical), then a 10-30 minute technical interview, with HR either separate or folded in.

### Online test

Sections on aptitude, logical reasoning, English grammar and mainly core mechanical at GATE level.

No specific questions reported.

### Technical/HR interview

10 to 30 minutes, focused on the specific profile.

**Thermal CFD profile questions**

CFD, heat transfer, fundamental laws and governing equations, turbulence modelling, numerical analysis and modelling, and practical thermal management such as engine cooling. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Tips:** Used GATE notes.

## 10 Nov 2025 · Associate Engineer, Analysis and Simulation · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Written test with technical and aptitude questions, then a technical interview of 15 to 40 minutes.

### Written test

Mix of technical and aptitude questions.

**Aptitude, reasoning and design/FEA/CFD questions**

Basic aptitude and reasoning plus technical questions on design and FEA/CFD. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Mechanical</sub>

### Technical interview

15 to 40 minutes.

**FEA/CFD basics and design decisions**

Fundamentals of the candidate's specialisation (FEA or CFD) and situational questions on which design decisions to take to reach desired results. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Tips:** Keep FEA/CFD basics clear and be able to justify the simulation settings you used in projects.

Source: [SWG Foresight, IIT Kharagpur](https://medium.com/@swgiitkgp/foresight-2025-placement-at-caterpillar-spandan-samanta-2930b6082800)
