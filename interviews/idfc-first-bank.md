## 10 Nov 2025 · Data Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV shortlisting, an online test on Unstop with three sections, then two interview rounds of about 2.5 hours in total.

### Online test

On Unstop, three sections: aptitude, recorded speech assessment, psychometric.

**Aptitude, speech and psychometric sections**

Section A tested problem-solving, logical reasoning and quantitative skills; Section B asked for recorded answers to role-based questions; Section C was a psychometric test. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Verbal, HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Interview 1

With junior IDFC colleagues; neither strictly technical nor HR.

**Past work deep dive**

Deep dive into past work, especially a bank internship, plus a case discussion on how the candidate approaches data problems. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral, Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Interview 2

Five senior panellists.

**Case-based scenarios**

Multiple scenarios to break down into data points and customer journeys, plus discussion of thought process, learning attitude and problem solving. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Tips:** Know your CV inside out, as it is grilled heavily. · Practise Python, SQL and Excel, and open-ended product and metric cases.
