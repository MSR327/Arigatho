## 22 Sep 2026 · TCS Prime · on-campus

TCS NQT online test, one combined technical-managerial interview, one HR.

### TCS NQT

180 minutes; aptitude, verbal, reasoning, coding (2 easy-medium array questions)

**Coding section**

Two easy-to-medium array and logic problems. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Arrays</sub>

### Technical and managerial interview

About 30 minutes, offline

**Print primes from 1 to 100**

Write a program to generate primes up to 100 and explain the efficient approach.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

**C++ and OOP**

Classes, encapsulation, abstraction, inheritance, polymorphism, overloading vs overriding, basic STL.

<sub>Topics: OOP</sub>

**Second highest salary**

SQL query for the second highest salary, plus basic DBMS concepts.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

**OS, networks, Git, Docker**

Process vs thread, context switching, OSI layers, Git commands, Docker image vs container, Kubernetes pods.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ, Software Engineering</sub>

**Project deep-dive**

Technologies, architecture, APIs, databases and challenges in resume projects.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR

Offline

**Background and motivation**

Background, motivation, career plans, willingness to work under company terms.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

## 23 Feb 2026 · System Engineer - Digital · on-campus

TCS NQT shortlist, then screening, technical, managerial and HR interviews on one day.

### Screening

About 10 to 15 minutes, in person

**Intro and hobbies**

Introduce yourself, hobbies, non-academic achievements.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Technical

About 25 to 30 minutes, in person

**Python and Java fundamentals**

Python lists vs arrays; can Python code run elsewhere without an interpreter; compiled vs interpreted; similar discussion on Java.

<sub>Topics: Code Output (C/C++/Java/Python)</sub>

**JOIN with UPDATE**

A DBMS query combining JOIN with UPDATE.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

### Managerial

About 20 minutes

**Final-year project**

Detailed project explanation, team lead role, use of Pandas.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral, Python & Pandas</sub>

**Metadata and false positive rate**

Meaning of metadata; what is False Positive Rate, especially in multi-class classification.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### HR

About 10 to 15 minutes

**Relocation and shifts**

Willingness to relocate, comfort with night shifts, document verification.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Revise DBMS JOIN and UPDATE logic. · Know your project and ML metrics conceptually. · Be flexible on relocation and shifts.
