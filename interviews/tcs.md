## 23 Feb 2026 · System Engineer - Digital · on-campus

TCS NQT shortlist, then screening, technical, managerial and HR interviews on one day.

### Screening

About 10 to 15 minutes, in person

**Intro and hobbies**

Introduce yourself, hobbies, non-academic achievements.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Technical

About 25 to 30 minutes, in person

**Python and Java fundamentals**

Python lists vs arrays; can Python code run elsewhere without an interpreter; compiled vs interpreted; similar discussion on Java.

<sub>Topics: Code Output (C/C++/Java/Python)</sub>

**JOIN with UPDATE**

A DBMS query combining JOIN with UPDATE.

<sub>Topics: SQL</sub>

### Managerial

About 20 minutes

**Final-year project**

Detailed project explanation, team lead role, use of Pandas.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral, Python & Pandas</sub>

**Metadata and false positive rate**

Meaning of metadata; what is False Positive Rate, especially in multi-class classification.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### HR

About 10 to 15 minutes

**Relocation and shifts**

Willingness to relocate, comfort with night shifts, document verification.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Revise DBMS JOIN and UPDATE logic. · Know your project and ML metrics conceptually. · Be flexible on relocation and shifts.
