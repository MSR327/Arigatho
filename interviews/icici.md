## 2025-26 season · Manager -1 · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

An online assessment of about 60 MCQs in one hour, then a single resume-driven interview.

### Online assessment

About 60 MCQs in 1 hour covering OOP, programming languages, ML, SQL, reasoning and aptitude.

No specific questions reported.

### Interview

Single round of 40 to 50 minutes, heavily resume-driven.

**Resume, projects and internship**

Detailed questions on projects, internships and technical concepts listed on the resume; much of it was about a backend internship.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**HR and bank basics**

A few HR questions and basic questions on ICICI Bank, its services and the banking industry.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Clear the online assessment first; there is no extended shortlist or walk-in. · Know every project, internship and skill on the resume. · Prepared ML, SQL and DSA broadly using Striver, a university ML course and SQL practice.

## 28 Sep 2025 · Manager-1 (Data Science & Analytics) · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Proctored online MCQ test, a psychometric test, then one interview (no GD).

### Online test

HackerEarth, proctored, 45 MCQs in 45 minutes; 148 students sat it.

**Mixed MCQs**

MCQs on aptitude, ML, Python, JS, OOP, SQL, OS and other CS topics. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, ML/AI, OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

### Psychometric test

100 behavioural questions, 30 to 40 minutes; 50 students shortlisted.

No specific questions reported.

### Interview

30 to 35 minutes, two-member panel, conversational.

**Introduction, CV walkthrough and college journey**

Self-introduction, CV walkthrough and a brief on college journey, with follow-ups.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Projects and banking current affairs**

Questions on projects and current affairs in the banking sector.

<sub>Topics: Finance, HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Stock market, digital payments, UPI UX**

Discussion on the stock market, digital payments and improving user experience of UPI apps.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**PORs**

Positions of responsibility.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Sales situational task**

A simple sales-based situational task. Exact task not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Build fundamentals in ML, analytics, SQL and aptitude. · Know every CV point and communicate clearly.
