## 11 Nov 2025 · Analyst-2 (Business Analyst) · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

CV submission, online assessment, then two case-style interviews.

### Online assessment

Logical reasoning, aptitude and data interpretation, depending on chosen profile.

**Reasoning, aptitude and data interpretation**

Questions on logical reasoning, aptitude and data interpretation. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude</sub>

### Interview 1: market entry case

**Factors before investing in a new business**

Interviewer described a hypothetical company planning a new business for a target market; asked what to consider before investing, such as market size and development cost.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Revenue estimate for a region**

Narrow down the target market and estimate revenue for that region using logical assumptions.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Revenue over different years**

Calculate revenue in different years based on conditions given by the interviewer.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases, Finance</sub>

**Breakeven costs or units**

Compute breakeven costs or units under the interviewer's conditions.

<sub>Topics: Finance, Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Interview 2: campaign effectiveness case

**Best advertising campaign combination**

Given a table of online and offline ad campaigns reaching different numbers of people, identify defining parameters (clicks, orders, enquiries) and find the most effective combination across channels.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases, Other</sub>

**Tips:** Practise reasoning, aptitude and DI for speed and accuracy. · Practise guesstimates and finance cases from casebooks. · Learn basic cost management finance; some BA interviews also ask SQL/Python.
