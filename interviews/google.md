## 14 Sep 2025 · Software Development Engineer · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Three interviews, each with a 45-minute coding question and 15 minutes of Googlyness.

### Interviews 1 to 3

Hybrid, shared document for code; each 45 min coding plus 15 min Googlyness; medium to hard.

**Medium-to-hard coding questions**

Coding questions mostly on arrays, strings, recursion, DP, greedy, graphs and binary search; code cleanliness assessed. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Arrays, Strings, DP</sub>

**Googlyness questions**

Questions on being a team player, valuing yourself and reacting in different situations. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Focus on DSA and complexity analysis; no OS, CA or DBMS asked. · Do contests for speed. · Communicate and ask for hints when stuck.
