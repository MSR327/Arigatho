## 9 Oct 2025 · Management Trainee (Data Science, Business Analytics and AI/ML) · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Screening test, then two interviews on the same day.

### Screening test

Easy to moderate.

**Maths, probability, LR, DI and ML test**

Test covering basic mathematics, probability, logical reasoning, data interpretation and machine learning. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Math, ML/AI</sub>

### Interview 1

CV-based questions plus a case.

**Market entry case**

A fairly simple market entry case: lay out potential entry paths for a firm into a new business.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

**Thesis on NLP**

Explain Transformer architecture and encoder-decoder setup, and sketch the proposed model with its neural network layers on paper.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

### Interview 2

Deep dive into ML applied to business problems.

**Project extension to card fraud detection**

Brainstorm on the spot how a project extends to credit card fraud detection.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Bias in ML models**

Discussion of biases in machine learning models.

<sub>Topics: ML/AI</sub>

**Why AMEX and company business**

Why American Express, and what do you know about its business.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Python syntax and SQL**

Other candidates were reportedly asked basic Python syntax and SQL. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: SQL, Python & Pandas</sub>

**Tips:** Practice CAT-style mocks, probability and statistics, and revise ML with numerical problems. · Do case practice with peers using casebooks, focusing on financial services cases. · Read the company website.
