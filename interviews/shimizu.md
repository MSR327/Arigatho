## 2025-26 season · Civil Engineer · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Resume-based shortlisting, then one online interview with a panel of three technical members and one HR member.

### Resume shortlisting

No specific questions reported.

### Online interview

Panel of 3 technical and 1 HR; friendly; centred on the candidate's prior construction work experience.

**Construction site problems and resolutions**

What kinds of problems have you faced on a construction site and how did you resolve them?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Construction drawings**

What kinds of drawings are used in construction and how do you read them?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Execution under a hypothetical breakdown**

How would you manage execution under a given hypothetical breakdown situation?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**NDT on a construction site**

What is NDT, and what are its applications and requirements on a construction site?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Contracts**

What is a contract and what are the different parts of a contract?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Tips:** Read the proforma and JD carefully before applying, since accepting a job ends your placement process. · Use civil handbooks and know your work responsibilities. · Present yourself as confident, positive and honest.
