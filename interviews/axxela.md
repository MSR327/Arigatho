## 23 Nov 2025 · Derivatives Trading Analyst · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Computer-based speed math and aptitude test, group trading simulation, technical interview with a trader, HR interview.

### Computer-based test

Speed math section of 6 minutes and aptitude section of 24 minutes.

**Speed mathematics section**

30 fill-in-the-blank questions in 6 minutes: 3x3 digit multiplication, division and decimal arithmetic.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, Math</sub>

**Aptitude section**

23 aptitude questions (logical reasoning and analytical problems) in 24 minutes. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude</sub>

### Group trading simulation

Group trading game with six internal rounds; two of six participants per group advanced.

**Currency-note digit-sum trading game**

Participants predicted and traded based on the sum of digits on currency notes over six internal rounds, and explained the reasoning behind their trades.

<sub>Topics: Finance, Guesstimates & Cases</sub>

### Technical interview with trader

One-on-one with a trader of 5+ years experience, rapid-fire style.

**Resume deep-dive on hedging project**

Discussion of projects, particularly derivatives-based hedging work.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Portfolio, risk and derivatives**

Questions on portfolio management, risk assessment and derivatives. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Speed math and logic puzzles**

Mental maths challenges and logical puzzles asked quickly. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Puzzles, Aptitude</sub>

**Memory test**

Recall a sequence of digits and letters shown at the start of the interview.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Risk appetite hypothetical**

A hypothetical about jumping from a roof, used to gauge risk appetite.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR interview

Personality and culture fit.

**Risk tolerance and loss handling**

Questions on approach to calculated risk, dealing with losses, interest in trading and rational decision making, plus extra memory and math tests. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practise speed maths daily without a calculator. · Know your finance projects thoroughly; Hull's derivatives book recommended. · Practise puzzles and answering quickly in a structured way.

## 5 Oct 2025 · Trainee Analyst (trading) · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Aptitude and personality test, trading game, then two interviews (finance and mental maths, stress test).

### Aptitude test

6 questions each with 6 quick-maths subparts, plus a personality section.

**Quick maths subparts**

Attempt as many quick maths subparts as possible, choosing ones solvable in seconds. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, OA Format</sub>

**Personality and behaviour section**

General questions about personality and behaviour. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### Trading game

Game played to earn maximum profit; performance decided interview shortlist.

No specific questions reported.

### Interview 1

20-30 minutes.

**Finance basics and CV**

Basic finance questions and anything finance-related on the resume.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Call option price formula**

Several candidates were asked for the Black-Scholes-Merton call option price formula.

<sub>Topics: Finance</sub>

**Memory test**

Memorise a sequence such as a 10-character alphanumeric string or a 12-15 digit number in 5-10 seconds, then recall it after 10-15 minutes, written from the last digit.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Mental maths: 11^82**

Compute 11 to the power 82 mentally; also squares of 2-3 digit numbers and cubes of 2-digit numbers.

<sub>Topics: Math</sub>

### Interview 2 (stress test)

20-30 minutes.

**Mental calculation and trader fit**

Rapid mental calculation, quick maths, and why you are a good fit to be a trader, answered with a trader's mindset.

<sub>Topics: Math, HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Memory test**

Memorisation questions again appear in this round.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Tips:** Practise fast mental maths and squares/cubes · Train memory on 12-15 digit sequences (first six and last six) · Know finance basics and anything finance on your CV

Source: [SWG Foresight, IIT Kharagpur](https://medium.com/@swgiitkgp/foresight-2025-placement-at-axxela-aditya-ravte-f115b831f2a1)
