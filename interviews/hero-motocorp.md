## 2025-26 season · Post Graduate Engineer Trainee · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Aptitude and technical test with elimination, resume shortlisting, then a technical interview and an HR interview.

### Aptitude and technical test

Elimination-based; important subjects were SOM, heat transfer, TOM and fluid mechanics.

No specific questions reported.

### Technical interview

Mostly based on thesis, B.Tech project, course projects, work experience and internships.

**M.Tech thesis**

Describe your M.Tech thesis or project and explain how it is relevant to the company and its operations.
