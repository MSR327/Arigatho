## 2025-26 season · SDE · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

A long test, then a single one-hour interview in an unusual format.

### Online test

Long and tedious, held 2-3 days before Day 1; MCQs on ethics and general topics plus LeetCode-style coding.

No specific questions reported.

### Interview

One round of 1 hour; the interviewer wrote down what the candidate said on paper.

**Explain all resume projects and POR**

Explain every project and position of responsibility on the resume.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**A LeetCode problem chosen from the interviewer's phone**

Solve a LeetCode problem the interviewer looked up, writing code on paper and explaining it. Exact problem not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**SQL vs NoSQL and relational databases**

Difference between SQL and NoSQL databases and what relational databases are, in depth.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**Deadlock and resolving a thread cycle**

Where deadlocks occur, and write code to resolve a cycle of two threads dependent on each other's output.

<sub>Topics: OS/DBMS/CN MCQ</sub>

**OOP concepts and trade-offs**

Basic uses, advantages and disadvantages of polymorphism, inheritance and related OOP concepts.

<sub>Topics: OOP</sub>

## 2025-26 season · Software Engineer · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Placement test, then a single interview combining introduction, resume, DSA and HR; about 30 shortlisted and 4 selected.

### Placement test

Mental ability and reasoning questions (CAT-like) plus three easy coding questions; speed mattered.

No specific questions reported.

### Interview

One combined round.

**Introduction and Natwest knowledge**

Self-introduction, why join Natwest and what you know about its work; speaking skills mattered.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Resume and software internship**

Questions on the resume and a software internship.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**DSA problems**

Five to six DSA questions, mostly medium and one hard. Exact problems not given.

<sub>Topics: Arrays</sub>

**Why this field and why hire you**

Why this field and why should we hire you.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>
