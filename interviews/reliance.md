## 2025-26 season · Graduate Engineer Trainee · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Resume application, a roughly 90-minute core civil technical test, then a combined HR plus technical interview of about an hour.

### Technical test

About 90 minutes, around 100 core civil questions; easy but time-pressured.

**Core civil MCQs**

About 100 questions on structures, civil engineering materials, RCC, geotechnical engineering, geoinformatics, steel and construction management. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

### HR + technical interview

About one hour combining core civil, resume and HR questions.

**Civil materials, RCC and IS codes**

Questions on civil engineering materials and RCC, and which IS codes apply, such as IS 456:2000, IS 383 and IS 800.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Internship and project walkthrough**

Explain in detail the civil operations internship work and the projects on the resume; many follow-up questions.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Knowledge of Reliance and HR questions**

What do you know about Reliance and the sectors it operates in; why join Reliance; do you want higher education; family background; willingness to work in remote locations and in a core field.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Prepared from previous course notes and GATE/ESE one-shot revision videos; questions are simple but theory must be strong. · Start core preparation early and do mock interviews and resume-explaining sessions.

## 2025-26 season · Manager / Team Member (Mechanical) · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Resume shortlisting, a technical interview and an HR interview.

### Resume shortlisting

No specific questions reported.

### Technical interview

Core mechanical concepts, MTech thesis details, and practical awareness of manufacturing and supply chain.

**MTech thesis in detail**

Explain the MTech thesis or project in detail.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Project challenges**

What challenges did you face in the project and how did you overcome them?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Fatigue failure**

What is fatigue failure?

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

**Supply chain management**

What is supply chain management, why is it important in manufacturing, what are the key stages of a supply chain, and how can disruptions affect production and project timelines?

<sub>Topics: Mechanical</sub>

### HR interview

Assessed communication, willingness to work at project locations, teamwork, leadership and career goals.

**Standard HR questions**

Tell me about yourself; are you willing to work at project locations across India; why should we hire you?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Revise core mechanical subjects (thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, heat transfer, machine design, manufacturing). · Prepare concise explanations of every project and internship. · Do mock interviews and do not neglect HR preparation.

## 2025-26 season · Team Member / Project Manager · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Direct resume shortlisting, then a technical interview and an HR interview.

### Resume shortlisting

Direct shortlisting based on CPI, with some exceptions.

No specific questions reported.

### Technical interview

About 30-40 minutes; interviewers say the role is a poor fit to test how flexible you are.

**Thesis and work-experience questions**

Mostly questions based on the candidate's thesis and work experience. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Process safety**

What is process safety?

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Project management and role tasks**

What do you understand by project management, and what tasks would you do in this role?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

### HR interview

About 15-20 minutes.

**Position-of-responsibility questions**

Questions drawn from past positions of responsibility, such as recommending a book (from a book club secretary role) and the key qualities of a manager.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** The thesis drives over half of the interview questions. · Be clear how your project benefits the company and role. · If you do not know something, say so honestly rather than guessing.
