## 2025-26 season · Management Trainee · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Resume shortlisting, a 90-120 minute technical test with core, reasoning, verbal and aptitude sections, then a combined technical plus HR interview of 45-60 minutes.

### Resume shortlisting

Author thinks it is based mostly on CPI and internship or core project experience.

No specific questions reported.

### Technical test

90 or 120 minutes, 3 to 4 sections: core, reasoning, verbal, aptitude; easy short questions with little time.

**Core civil section**

Questions on civil engineering materials, structures, RCC, geotechnical, geoinformatics, steel and construction management. Exact questions not given.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

### Technical + HR interview

45-60 minutes with a panel of 3 technical members and 1 HR.

**Internship and project explanation**

Explain the civil operations internship and resume projects in detail, with follow-up clarifications.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Core civil questions**

Questions mainly on structures, RCC and civil materials, going deep into theory such as percentage composition of components in concrete mixes and admixtures like fly ash.

<sub>Topics: Other</sub>

**Argue a position on taxation and special treatment**

HR gave a topic and opposed the candidate: since the rich pay proportionally more tax, should they get special benefits and treatment when using services? The candidate had to defend a view against pushback.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Family background**

Questions about family members and their professions.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>
