No specific questions reported.

## 30 Oct 2025 · Business Analyst Associate · IIT Kharagpur · on-campus

Aptitude-style online test, then three quantitative case interviews and one HR round.

### Online assessment

23 MCQs in 30 minutes on the Nalanda platform.

**Data interpretation MCQs**

23 MCQs mostly on data interpretation from bar charts, line charts, tables and candlestick patterns.

<sub>Topics: Aptitude, OA Format</sub>

### Case interviews (3 rounds)

Three rounds, no calculators; cases were more quantitative than consulting-style.

**Business case turned into maths**

A business situation unfolds like a consulting case, then must be translated into a mathematical problem and solved with supplied data points, without a calculator. Exact cases not given.

<sub>Topics: Guesstimates & Cases, Math</sub>

### HR round

Three open-ended qualitative questions.

**Time you failed**

Talk about a time you failed and how you dealt with it. Two other open-ended questions not specified.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Practise aptitude (e.g. IndiaBix) and mental maths · Prepare consulting-style cases · Keep a document of standard HR questions with personal stories
