## 2025-26 season · Software Development Engineer -I · IIT Kanpur · on-campus

Online assessment (main filter), then two interviews: an implementation problem-solving round and an internship deep dive with HR questions.

### Online assessment

Critical filter for interview shortlisting.

No specific questions reported.

### Round 1

Implementation-based problem solving; logic and methodology mattered more than perfect syntax.

**Order content with creator spacing**

Given an array of (creator_id, content_id), reorder so content from the same creator is at least d positions apart. Author says it resembles a LeetCode problem on rearranging a string with k distance apart.

<sub>Topics: Greedy, Heaps</sub>

### Round 2

Deep dive into the internship, plus technical, situational and HR questions.

**Internship deep dive**

What you did, how you did it, and the impact.

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Why Meesho**

Why do you want to join Meesho?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Internship feedback**

What feedback did the internship company give?

<sub>Topics: HR & Behavioral</sub>

**Tips:** Prepare standard HR questions and a clear self-introduction script. · Do not put projects on the resume that you cannot defend. · Cover DSA (graphs, DP, greedy, linked lists), SQL/DBMS and ML fundamentals.
