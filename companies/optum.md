### Q1. Optum AI-assisted coding OA

The OA was an AI-assisted coding round. A codebase was given. You could choose JS or Python and, using an AI chatbot, implement a few features within an hour. The AI assistant provided almost all of the answers.

## 2026-11-10 · NIT Warangal · FTE · Interview

### Q1. Optum HR and technical interview

HR interview: basic HR questions, about 10-15 minutes. Final technical round varied by interviewer: some asked in depth about the entire resume, others asked about things not on it. DSA was basic or medium. The focus was on projects, CS fundamentals, critical thinking and knowledge of AI. Some system design questions were also asked.
