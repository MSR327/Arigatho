## 2026-11-08 · IIT BHU · FTE · Interview

### Q1. Sirius AI interview rounds

Sirius AI skips online tests and goes straight to three interview rounds:

- Round 1: Resume projects, basic Python/SQL, and logic puzzles.
- Round 2: Role-specific deep dive. Data science roles focus on classic ML models (XGBoost, Random Forests) plus guesstimates. Data engineering tracks test SQL optimization, PySpark and GCP.
- Round 3: Cultural fit and behavioral chat with the co-founder.
