## 2026-11-07 · IIT BHU · FTE · Interview

### Q1. Interview format with business case study and guesstimate

One-hour interview conducted by a company employee. Began with a self-introduction, then questions on academic background, experiences and projects.

Main focus was a business case study: given a scenario about a company's performance, analyze the information, interpret data (charts, tables, key business metrics), identify possible causes of business challenges, recommend solutions, compare multiple approaches and justify the chosen one.

The interview also included a guesstimate question (the exact question was not shared); emphasis was on approach and assumptions rather than the final number. The interviewer asked many follow-up questions.
