## 2026-11-19 · IIT BHU · FTE

### Q1. Trademo OA pattern and sections

Trademo OA is a 60-to-90-minute proctored test split into four main sections.

- Aptitude & Reasoning: 15–20 MCQs covering percentages, ratios, profit and loss, and basic chart interpretation.
- SQL Querying: 2–3 live coding questions and 5 MCQs focused on JOINs, GROUP BY, CTEs, and window functions.
- Excel & Data Cleaning: 10–15 scenario MCQs testing VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, and Pivot Tables.
- Statistics & Python: 5–10 basic MCQs on mean, median, probability, and simple pandas operations.
