### Q2. Split array into k segments, pair-count cost

Divide the array into k contiguous segments. The cost of a segment is the number of pairs (i, j) in it with i < j and a[i] - a[j] >= D. Find the best total cost over all ways to split (from memory; the objective is presumably the minimum total cost).

Topics: DP, Arrays
