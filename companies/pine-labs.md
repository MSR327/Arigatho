### Q1. Pine Labs online assessment format

The Pine Labs online assessment is a two-part test:

- Part 1: Multiple-choice questions testing speed on basic math, logic puzzles and English reading.
- Part 2: A coding section on HackerEarth. Problems range from medium to tough, with topics like stock span, array manipulation, complex linked list logic and dynamic programming. Edge cases and optimization matter for hidden test cases.
