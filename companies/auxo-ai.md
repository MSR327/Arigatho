## 2026-11-07 · IIT Hyderabad · FTE · Interview

### Q1. Business case study and guesstimate round

Interview conducted by a company employee, about an hour. Began with a brief self-introduction and a few questions on background.

Main part: a business case study. Given a scenario involving a company's performance, the candidate had to analyze the information, interpret data, identify possible causes of business issues, and recommend solutions. The interviewer expected the candidate to:
- Analyze charts, tables, and metrics.
- Draw logical conclusions from the data.
- Justify recommendations with clear reasoning.
- Compare alternative approaches and explain choices.

There was also a guesstimate question to evaluate structured thinking and assumptions; the focus was on framework and thought process rather than the exact number. (The specific case and guesstimate were not given.)
