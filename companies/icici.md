## 2026-10-13 · MNIT Jaipur · FTE

### Q1. ICICI DP OA overall pattern

ICICI DP OA (MNIT) – Overall pattern:

1. OA mainly covered MCQs from Python, C/C++, C#, R, SQL/DBMS, Networking and basic programming.
2. Python questions included tuples, decorators, tokenization, NumPy arrays, OOP and output prediction.
3. OOP questions covered inheritance, polymorphism, abstract classes and method overriding/hiding.
4. ML/NLP questions included boosting, weak learners, Latent Semantic Indexing (LSI) and parsing.
5. Data Science questions covered regression, scatterplots, probability, boxplots and linear algebra.
6. DBMS questions included deadlock prevention (Wait-Die/Wound-Wait) and DROP INDEX.
7. Computer Networks questions covered unicast, broadcasting, IP headers, UDP, DNS and DHCP snooping.
8. Linux/DNS-related questions included hostname and IP lookup commands.
9. Aptitude questions included train–bridge speed calculations and percentage comparisons.
10. Agile/Cloud questions covered Scrum, Kanban, PySpark and BigQuery.

Overall, prepare broad technical MCQs, code-output questions and basic aptitude rather than focusing on DSA alone.
