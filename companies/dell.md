## 2026-11-12 · IIIT Bangalore · FTE · Other round

### Q1. Dell process summary

Dell interviews are mostly 3 to 4 rounds.
- Technical round: DSA (arrays, trees, linked list, DP, graphs, binary search, two sum, Kadane's, topological sort) + OOPS + DBMS/SQL + OS + CN + project discussion.
- One experience had an OA with 2 DSA coding problems + CS MCQs before interviews.
- Final round is HR with standard behavioral questions (why Dell, conflict situations).
