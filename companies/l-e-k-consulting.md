## 2026-11-02 · BITS Pilani · FTE

### Q1. L.E.K. OA pattern

The test is fairly standard. From what the poster heard (late 2025), it is heavier on data questions than most consulting tests, such as graphs and thinking in terms of growth or margin percentages. Be able to read common graph types and quickly calculate answers from them.

Expect some multiple-choice situational questions, e.g. "In your team, you have one person behaving in way X and another in way Y; you notice some tension. How would you manage this?"

The poster said the tests change fairly often, with questions or types rotated every 1-2 years.
