### Q9. Reverse string keeping spaces + word count

You are given a string N. Your task is to reverse the string while keeping the spaces in their original positions, followed by the total count of the words in the string.

Input: a single line containing the string N.
Constraints: 1 <= N <= 10^6
Output: a single line containing the modified string, and the word count at the end.

Sample Input: my name is doselect
Sample Output: tc eles od siemanym 4

Explanation: Reverse the string without moving the spaces. There is one space after "my", so after reversing, "my" becomes "tc", "name" becomes "eles", "is" becomes "od", "doselect" becomes "siemanym". 4 is the word count.
Execution time limit: 10 seconds.

Topics: Strings, Two Pointers  
📎 source: [InfoEdge.pdf](../archive/files/2461-InfoEdge.pdf)

Sample 1: Input 2 2 2 4 4 -> Output 4 (the knight can move to 4 different positions without being attacked by the queen).
