## 2026-11-08 · IIT BHU · FTE · Quant Analyst

### Q1. Goldman Sachs Quant Analyst OA pattern

HackerRank OA of around 97 minutes, in three parts:

- Software Fundamentals: data structures, algorithms and complexity.
- Software Diagnostics: debugging codebases (such as Django, Spring Boot or Node.js). A built-in, limited AI assistant is allowed here; external AI tools and extensions are banned.
- Quantitative Math: MCQs on probability, statistics, linear algebra and calculus.
