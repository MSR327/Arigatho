## 2026-11-06 · Unknown · FTE · Software

### Q1. Kanini OA and LWS round format

Duration: 2 hours.

Format: 40 MCQs (Aptitude and Fundamentals) and 6 coding questions (easy-to-medium, ~15 minutes per question).

Cutoff: 80% or higher to be shortlisted.

Round 2: LWS (Listening, Writing & Speaking)
1. Listening test: an audio track is played, followed by 10 questions; need 8/10 (80%) to move forward.
2. Writing test: given a scenario, write a response.
3. Speaking test.
