### Q1. LTM OA format: behaviour, speaking, coding, essay, rating

LTM OA mainly checks English skills and behaviour. It has 5 sections:

1. Choose the best and worst decisions you can take in a given situation (behavioural).
2. Reading a sentence aloud, plus listening and reading aloud.
3. Coding: 2 questions, which can be done in about 5 minutes.
4. Essay writing (20 minutes).
5. Rate a situation from 1 to 5 based on your liking.

The actual questions were not shared.
