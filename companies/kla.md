### Q1. Ball bag reduction: last ball left

A bag has 25 white balls and 50 black balls, plus an infinite pile of black balls outside. At each step you take 2 balls from the bag (without replacement):

- If both are black (BB): throw one black away and put the other back.
- If one is white and one is black (WB or BW): throw the black away and put the white back.
- If both are white (WW): throw both away and add one black ball from the pile.

At every step the number of balls in the bag decreases by one. What colour is the last ball when only one ball is left?
