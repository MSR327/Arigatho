### Q3. Food delivery commission for breakeven in 1 year

Super Eats is planning to diversify into the food delivery business. The company plans to use couriers in cars, motorcycles and cycles to collect food from restaurants and deliver it to consumers around the city. The company processes orders from customers and delivers them while collecting a commission (% of revenue) from the restaurants for providing the order. Additional information: cost of acquisition of customers through social media marketing is Rs. 1000 per customer; on average, a customer places 2 orders per month with an average order value of Rs. 250; average cost to deliver is Rs. 60 per delivery for the company; the company charges the customer Rs. 20 per delivery currently. What should be the commission percentage that the company should demand from the restaurants if it wants to breakeven in 1 year?

A. 45%
B. 32.67%
C. 16%
D. 25%

Answer given: 32.67%

### Q6. Price elasticity of Product C

SMS Industries operates in beverages and confectionaries industry and sells three products A, B and C. SMS Group is a major client which buys 50%, 30% and 20% of the annual production capacities of products A, B and C respectively from SMS Industries. A table gives production capacity and selling price for each product. To meet increasing costs, SMS Industries plans to increase the prices of products A, B and C by 10%, 15% and 8% respectively and share the revised price schedule with its clients. Owing to the revised price schedule, SMS Group reduced their demand for products A, B and C to 45%, 30% and 15% of the respective annual production capacities. Assume there has been no change in production capacities or the operating capacities. What is the price elasticity (ratio of % change in volume to % change in price) of Product C, considering only SMS group's demand?

A. 0.80
B. 0.50
C. 1.15
D. 1.25

Answer given: 1.25. The product table is not legible.

D. 54.2%
