## 2026-11-06 · IIT Roorkee · FTE · Data & AI Analyst 2 / AI/ML Engineer 2

### Q1. Accordion OA pattern

Data & AI Analyst 2: 1 section, PST Section, 20 minutes.

AI/ML Engineer 2: 3 sections:
- PST Section: 20 minutes
- Data Science Conceptual Section: 7 minutes
- Data Science Coding Section: 23 minutes
(The DS Conceptual section includes a few data science conceptual questions along with the coding section.)

PST: multiple-choice business problem-solving questions, 20 minutes, 15 problem-solving questions. Focus: numerical reasoning, structured thinking, basic business math. Marking: +1 for each correct answer, -0.5 for each wrong answer.
