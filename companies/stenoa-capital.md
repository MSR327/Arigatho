## 2026-10-26 · IIT Kanpur · FTE

### Q1. Minimum time to collect all points

You are given \(n\) players and \(m\) points on an infinite number line.

- players[i] denotes the initial position of the \(i\)-th player.
- points[j] denotes the position of the \(j\)-th point.

At each second, every player can move one unit to the left, one unit to the right, or stay at their current position. All players move simultaneously.

A point is considered collected when at least one player visits its position.

Find the minimum time required for all \(m\) points to be collected.

Constraints
- \(1 \le n,m \le 10^5\)
- Player and point positions are integers.
