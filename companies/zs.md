### Q1. ZS verbal assessment round format

Verbal assessment round had three parts:

1. Repeat what is said (repeat sentences).
2. Listen to a 60 second audio, then answer questions based on it.
3. Speak on a given topic for 60 seconds.

The actual sentences, audio questions and topics were not shared.
