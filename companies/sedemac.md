## 2026-11-07 · IIT Indore · FTE · Interview

### Q1. SEDEMAC Round 2 technical MCQ test format

Round 2 (Technical): 45 minutes, 15 multiple-choice questions. Topics: basic electronics (like RC circuits), SHM and fundamental physics, battery and power systems, engineering mathematics, mechanical engineering fundamentals, probability and statistics. Mostly JEE or first-year engineering core concepts.

### Q2. SEDEMAC Round 3 technical interview

Round 3: technical interview of about 60 to 70 minutes. It began with a self-introduction and a discussion of resume projects, followed by about 7 to 8 technical questions. Focus areas: analog and digital signal concepts, time period and waveform basics, fundamental electronics, mechanical design and reasoning, applying mathematics to real-world problems, basic programming logic. Round 1 was an aptitude round. Specific questions were not shared.
