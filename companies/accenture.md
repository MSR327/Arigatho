### Q1. Count numbers whose first and last digits sum to 9

### Problem Statement: Count Numbers Whose First and Last Digits Sum to 9

You are given a list of `N` integers. Your task is to count how many numbers in the list satisfy the following condition:

The sum of the **first digit and the last digit** of a number must be equal to `9`.

Print the total count of such numbers.

**Input Format:**

- The first line contains an integer `N`, representing the number of elements in the list.
- The second line contains `N` space-separated integers.

**Output Format:**

- Print a single integer representing the count of numbers whose first and last digits add up to 9.

**Example:**

Input:

```text
6
18 27 123 45 567 90
```

Output:

```text
4
```

**Explanation:**

- `18` → 1 + 8 = 9 ✓
- `27` → 2 + 7 = 9 ✓
- `123` → 1 + 3 = 4 ✗
- `45` → 4 + 5 = 9 ✓
- `567` → 5 + 7 = 12 ✗
- `90` → 9 + 0 = 9 ✓

Therefore, the count is **4**.
