## 2026-11-11 · Unknown · FTE · Interview

### Q1. NPS Prism hiring process rounds

Round 1 (Technical): Core programming and data manipulation, such as writing Python or PySpark code to process CSV files, basic-to-intermediate SQL queries, and project architecture discussions.

Round 2 (Deep Dive / Tech): In-depth evaluation of past projects, big data concepts, and domain-specific problem solving (e.g., GCP, machine learning pipelines, data quality).

Round 3 (Managerial / Scenario): Scenario-based troubleshooting, technical leadership or system design questions.

HR / Compensation Discussion.

Note: the poster said this was taken from a Glassdoor post by a friend, not their own experience.

## 2026-11-11 · IIT Roorkee · FTE · Analyst / Data / Business Analyst · Interview

### Q1. NPS Prism hiring process for Analyst roles

Reported by a friend who interviewed at NPS Prism. For Analyst / Data / Business Analyst roles:

1. Resume Shortlisting
2. Online Assessment / Excel Test: Excel, data interpretation, basic analytics.
3. Technical Round 1: SQL, Python, statistics, Excel, resume-based questions.
4. Technical / Case Round 2: Projects, problem-solving, business cases, guesstimates.
5. Managerial / Motivation Round: Role fit, communication, why NPS Prism.
