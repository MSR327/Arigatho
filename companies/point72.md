### Q1. Max profit from investing with daily capital

There is an array of capital costs and an array of PnLs for successive days. You start with 0 capital and every day you receive some amount; the balance carries over to successive days. If you decide to invest on a day, the balance reduces by capitalcost[i] and you earn a profit of pnl[i]. Find the maximum profit.
