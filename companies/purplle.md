## 2026-11-08 · IIT BHU · FTE · ALDI leadership role

### Q1. Purplle ALDI assessment pattern

The Purplle ALDI leadership role assessment focuses on business strategy, analytics and leadership potential rather than coding:

- Business analytics: graphs, profit margins, growth metrics, e-commerce data interpretation.
- Logical reasoning: quick puzzles, data sufficiency, analytical reasoning.
- Situational judgment: real-world e-commerce scenarios (project deadlines, operational bottlenecks, stakeholder conflict).
- Behavioral fit: a psychometric test mapping decision-making style to Purplle's startup culture.
